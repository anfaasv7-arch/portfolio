Where I’m heading

My background in AI and machine learning gave me the tools to make systems predict and decide. My research on collision-aware computation offloading for autonomous vehicles showed me that intelligence only matters if it acts correctly and in time on real hardware. That is why I want to study intelligent robotics: to connect learning-based perception and decision-making with embedded, real-time systems, from sensors and microcontrollers to autonomous platforms. I am now building the hardware and ROS 2 skills to complete that loop.

Published Patent Application – Co-Inventor

Predictive Collision-Aware Offloading Scheduler

A hybrid MEC and V2V framework for real-time collision avoidance in autonomous vehicles. A Random Forest model predicts time-to-collision about one second ahead, and a scheduler picks where to compute: the on-board unit, a neighbouring vehicle, a roadside unit, or the cloud.

Application no. 202641121869. Co-inventor with Dr. Theresa Cenate C.F., Atharva Dubey, and Mohammed Sharique M.

PythonRandom ForestV2XEdge computing
Prototype · 2026

ESP32 Heart Risk Monitor

An IoT prototype that reads pulse data from a heart-rate sensor on an ESP32, combines it with health details the user enters, and shows a percentage-based risk estimate in a Flutter app, with guidance on when to see a doctor.

A prototype only, not a clinical diagnostic tool.

ESP32FlutterSensorsMachine learning
Planned · 2026 to 2027

ROS 2 Robotic Arm

A 3-DOF arm with a servo gripper and ESP8266 control, modelled in URDF and simulated in Gazebo and RViz, with forward and inverse kinematics, trajectory generation, and MoveIt 2 planning, ending in a pick-and-place demo.

Not built yet. This is the roadmap.
